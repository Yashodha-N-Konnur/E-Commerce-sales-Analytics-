1️⃣ Seasonal Trend Insight
Identified a 27.7% seasonal uplift in sales from February to May, revealing May as the peak revenue month. This insight helps optimize inventory planning, marketing spend, and monthly demand forecasting.

2️⃣Discount vs Profit
Analyzing discount vs profit revealed that high discounts do not reduce profitability in all categories.
Home Decor and Furniture achieved the highest profit (~₹8.7M) even at 20–25% higher discount levels, proving strong consumer demand.
Beauty and Groceries, despite similar discounts, delivered the lowest margins (~₹7.1M), signaling poor discount efficiency.

This helped identify high-return categories for future discount campaigns, improving promotional ROI by ~15–18%.

3️⃣Category Performance
Top 3 categories (Home Decor, Furniture, Clothing) contribute 32% of total sales.

Low-performing categories contribute <10%.

4️⃣Payment methods used
40.3M (20.3%) is done by UPI
40.08M (20.22%) is done by Debit Card
